# UNO: Complete Game Rules and Strategies

## 1. Objective and Components
- **Objective**: Be the first player to play all cards in your hand in each round, scoring points based on cards left in opponents' hands[cite: 4]. The first player to reach 500 points wins[cite: 4].
- **Components (112 cards total)**:
  - 76 Number cards (0-9 in Red, Green, Blue, Yellow)[cite: 4].
  - 24 Action cards (8 Skip, 8 Reverse, 8 Draw Two - 2 of each per color)[cite: 4].
  - 8 Wild cards (4 standard Wild, 4 Wild Draw Four)[cite: 4].
  - 4 Blank cards (for replacements or custom house rules)[cite: 4].

## 2. Game Setup
- Each player draws a card; the highest point value deals[cite: 4]. 
- Shuffle and deal 7 cards to each player[cite: 4]. Place the remaining cards face-down as a **DRAW pile**[cite: 4]. Turn over the top card to start a **DISCARD pile** (if it's a Wild or Wild Draw 4, return it and pick another)[cite: 4].

## 3. Gameplay
- The player to the left of the dealer goes first; play proceeds to the left[cite: 4]. 
- On your turn, match the top card of the DISCARD pile by **number, color, or symbol**[cite: 4]. If you have nothing that matches, you must draw one card from the DRAW pile[cite: 4]. If playable, you may play it immediately; otherwise, turn ends[cite: 4].

## 4. Special Cards and Actions
- **Skip Card**: The next person in line loses their turn[cite: 4].
- **Reverse Card**: Reverses the direction of play (clockwise becomes counter-clockwise)[cite: 4].
- **Draw Two Card**: The next person must draw 2 cards and forfeit their turn[cite: 4].
- **Wild Card**: Allows you to change the color being played to any color. Can be played at any time[cite: 4].
- **Wild Draw Four Card**: Allows you to call the next color, forcing the next player to draw 4 cards and forfeit their turn[cite: 4]. 
  - *Strict Restriction*: Can ONLY be played when you do **not** have a card in your hand matching the current color of the DISCARD pile[cite: 4].

## 5. The "UNO" Rule and Penalties
- Before playing your next-to-last card, you must yell **"UNO"**[cite: 4]. 
- If you forget and another player catches you before the next player begins their turn (by drawing or playing), you must pick **4 penalty cards** from the DRAW pile[cite: 4].
- If you catch yourself before anyone else notices, you are safe[cite: 4].

## 6. Challenges and Edge Cases
- **Wild Draw Four Challenge**: If you suspect a player played a Wild Draw Four illegally (holding a matching color card), you can challenge them[cite: 4]. The challenged player must show their hand[cite: 4]. If guilty, they draw the 4 cards; if innocent, the challenger draws 4 cards PLUS 2 extra penalty cards (6 total)[cite: 4].
- **Depleted Draw Pile**: If the DRAW pile runs out, reshuffle the DISCARD pile to form a new one[cite: 4].

## 7. Scoring
Points for cards left in opponents' hands when a player goes out[cite: 4]:
- **Number Cards (0-9)**: Face Value[cite: 4]
- **Draw 2 / Reverse / Skip**: 20 Points each[cite: 4]
- **Wild / Wild Draw Four**: 50 Points each[cite: 4]
- **Winner**: First player to reach 500 points[cite: 4].

## 8. Two-Player, Partners, and Tournaments
- **Two Players**: Playing a Reverse or Skip card acts like a Skip, allowing you to immediately take another turn[cite: 4]. After a Draw 2 or Wild Draw Four, your opponent draws and play immediately returns to you[cite: 4].
- **Partners (4 Players)**: Sit across from your partner; when either goes out, combine opponent points for team score[cite: 4].
