# Werewolf

## Overview
Werewolf is a social deduction role-playing game for groups of 7 to 20+ players. The game revolves around the battle for survival between two main factions: Villagers (trying to find and eliminate the Werewolves) and Werewolves (trying to stay hidden and devour Villagers). The game is moderated by a Game Master, who knows all roles and orchestrates the game phases.

## Components and Roles
The game requires 1 Game Master and the remaining players are assigned secret roles. Here are the most common roles:

*   **Game Master:** The game moderator. Belongs to no faction, does not close their eyes at night, and is responsible for waking up specific roles to use their abilities.
*   **Villager:** Has no special abilities. Their task is to wake up during the day, discuss, deduce, and vote to lynch players suspected of being Werewolves. Faction: Villager.
*   **Werewolf:** At night, the Werewolves wake up together and silently agree on 1 player to bite (kill). During the day, Werewolves pretend to be Villagers to avoid being lynched. Faction: Werewolf.
*   **Seer:** Every night, the Seer is awakened by the Game Master and points to 1 player. The Game Master nods if that player is a Werewolf, or shakes their head if they are not. Faction: Villager.
*   **Bodyguard:** Every night, chooses to protect 1 player (can protect themselves). The protected player will not die if bitten by the Werewolves that night. Special rule: Cannot protect the same person two nights in a row. Faction: Villager.
*   **Witch:** Has 2 one-time-use potions: 1 healing potion (to save the person just bitten by Werewolves) and 1 poison potion (to kill 1 player). Every night, the Game Master shows the Witch who was bitten, and the Witch decides whether to use the healing potion, the poison potion, or do nothing. Faction: Villager.
*   **Hunter:** If the Hunter dies (bitten by Werewolves or lynched by Villagers), they must immediately take aim and shoot another player, bringing them down to the grave with them. Faction: Villager.
*   **Cupid:** Wakes up only once on the first night. Cupid chooses any 2 players to pair them as lovers. If 1 of the 2 dies, the other immediately dies of a broken heart. Faction: Villager (or 3rd Faction if pairing 1 Werewolf and 1 Villager).

## Setup
1.  **Choose Game Master:** One person volunteers to be the Game Master to moderate the game.
2.  **Distribute Cards:** The Game Master selects a number of role cards corresponding to the number of players. Typically, Werewolves make up about 1/3 of the total players. Example: 9 players could include 2 Werewolves, 1 Seer, 1 Bodyguard, and 5 Villagers.
3.  **Receive Roles:** The Game Master gives each person 1 card (or sends a secret message). Players look at their roles and keep them strictly secret.
4.  **Start:** All players close their eyes. The game begins with the First Night.

## Gameplay: Night Phase
When the Game Master announces "Night falls, everyone close your eyes," all players go to sleep. The Game Master sequentially calls specific roles to wake up in the following order (those not called must keep their eyes closed):
1.  **Cupid (Night 1 only):** Wakes up, pairs two people, goes to sleep. The Game Master taps the shoulders of the 2 paired players. These two wake up to see each other's faces, then close their eyes.
2.  **Bodyguard:** Wakes up, points to 1 player to protect tonight, then goes to sleep.
3.  **Werewolves:** The Werewolves wake up and look at each other. They silently agree on 1 target to kill. After confirming, the Werewolves go to sleep.
4.  **Witch:** Wakes up. The Game Master points to the person just bitten by the Werewolves. The Witch signals whether to use the healing potion. Do they want to use the poison to kill anyone else? Then goes to sleep.
5.  **Seer:** Wakes up, points to 1 person to investigate. The Game Master signals whether they are a Werewolf or not. The Seer goes to sleep.

## Gameplay: Day Phase
1.  **Wake up:** The Game Master calls "Day breaks, everyone open your eyes."
2.  **Announcement:** The Game Master announces who died last night (without revealing their roles, unless using optional rules). Dead players are no longer allowed to speak or participate in voting.
3.  **Discussion:** The surviving players have a few minutes to discuss, argue, and deduce who the Werewolves are. Lying is completely permitted.
4.  **Voting:** The Game Master asks everyone to point at the person they suspect the most. The player with the most votes is allowed to defend themselves.
5.  **Final Judgment:** Everyone gives a thumbs up (live) or thumbs down (die) to decide the fate of the suspected player. If the majority votes to die, that player is lynched (eliminated from the game) and reveals their true role (depending on the rules).
6.  **Repeat:** The game loops back to the Night Phase.

## Winning Conditions
The game ends when one of the following occurs:
*   **Villager Faction Wins:** All Werewolves are eliminated.
*   **Werewolf Faction Wins:** The number of living Werewolves equals the number of living Villagers (At this point, Werewolves can openly attack without being outvoted).
*   **3rd Faction (Lovers) Wins:** If Cupid pairs 1 Werewolf and 1 Villager, they become a 3rd faction. This couple only wins if they are the last 2 players alive in the game.
